# Labeling guide (fill separately, don't compare until both are done)

Read only the question. Don't look at the log's dates, responders or answers.

**Root cause** (pick one)
- `field_condition`: something found on site that the drawings did not anticipate (an existing footing, soil, a utility)
- `design_conflict`: two drawings, disciplines or building elements clash
- `missing_info`: the drawings or specs leave out a needed dimension, elevation or location
- `contractor_proposal`: the GC or a sub proposes a change (a substitution, an easier way to build) and asks for approval

**Priority** (your guess from the text)
- 1: work in the field is waiting on this, answer within a few days
- 2: needed soon, about a week
- 3: can wait two weeks

If you are torn between two root causes, write both in `notes` and pick the one that best
explains why the RFI was written. Disagreements get settled together and written down.
