05 · DEMO CONCEPTS

Four Demo Concepts

01

Demo 1 — Missing Reference Detection

Test whether the AI drafting assistant can identify and prompt for missing drawing, document, or specification references before an RFI is sent.

02

Demo 2 — Quantity and Unit Check

Test whether the AI drafting assistant can detect quantities that are missing units or otherwise unclear.

03

Demo 3 — Bundled Question Detection

Test whether the AI drafting assistant can identify double-barreled questions and split them into clearer individual asks.

04

Demo 4 — Undefined Abbreviation Check

Test whether the AI drafting assistant can identify undefined abbreviations and prompt the user to clarify or expand them.
