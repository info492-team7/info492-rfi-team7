The instructor asked us to explain how our proposed 10% reduction in clarity defects could prevent delays and how we would evaluate the accuracy of identified defects.

### 3. Improving priority classification
Mentioned in: 1 of 3 instructor feedback records.

## Criticism of Our Thesis and Evaluation

Our initial thesis proposes a 10% improvement, but our experiments have not yet demonstrated a measurable reduction in RFI processing time or project delays.

The instructor also emphasized the importance of real data and stakeholder validation.
